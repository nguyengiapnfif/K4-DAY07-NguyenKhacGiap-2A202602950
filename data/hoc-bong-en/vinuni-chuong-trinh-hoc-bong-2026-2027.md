# VINUNIVERSITY – UNDERGRADUATE SCHOLARSHIP PROGRAM ACADEMIC YEAR 2026-2027

December 10, 2025

**COMMITTED TO NURTURING FUTURE TALENTS WITH A GENEROUS SCHOLARSHIP FUND**

As the youngest and fastest university in the world to receive an overall QS 5-Star rating – and with strong investment from Vingroup – VinUniversity is entering a new phase of growth, breaking through to become an entrepreneurial university powered by science and technology, determined to reach the World’s Top 100 Universities.

For the academic year 2026–2027, VinUniversity continues to strongly affirm its mission to nurture future talent through a generous scholarship fund, supporting outstanding students in overcoming financial barriers to pursue their dreams and contribute to society.

#### SCHOLARSHIPS OF UP TO FULL TUITION

VinUni’s Scholarship Program offers various packages, starting from 50% of tuition fees. The highest scholarship level covers 100% of tuition and living expenses.

**EMPOWERING EVERY STUDENT: 35% TUITION SUBSIDY FROM VINGROUP**

From 2025 to 2030, all students enrolling at VinUniversity will be granted a 35% tuition subsidy, applied for the full duration of their studies.

**MERIT-BASED SCHOLARSHIPS**

From 50% up to Full-ride scholarships, Merit-based Scholarship: Awarded to applicants with outstanding academic performance and strong personal qualities – those who demonstrate aspiration and ambition, creative thinking and a proactive, resilient spirit who never give up on their dreams.

- President’s Excellence Scholarship: Full-ride scholarship (Covers 100% of tuition and living expenses).
- Provost’s Merit Scholarship: Covers 100% of tuition
- Dean’s Distinction Scholarship: Covers 80% and 90% of tuition.
- Discipline’s Honor Scholarship: Covers 50%, 60%, and 70% of tuition.

(\*) Merit-Based Scholarships already include the 35% tuition subsidy.

**SPECIAL ENCOURAGEMENT SCHOLARSHIPS (\*Stackable):**

- Special Academic Scholarship: Offers a 5% tuition waiver for selected academic disciplines, as designated for each academic year.
- WIT (Woman in Tech) Scholarship: Provides an additional 5% tuition support for female applicants pursuing studies in technology or science-related fields.
- Vinschool-VinUni Scholarship: Covers a 5% tuition waiver for Vinschool graduates who are admitted to VinUniversity.
- Vingroup Family Scholarship: Available for children of Vingroup employees and executives, as part of the Group’s commitment to fostering talent within its community.

**SPECIAL SPONSORED SCHOLARSHIPS FROM PRIVATE FUND**

This program supports exceptional applicants who have earned a Merit-based Scholarship (80% or higher) but still face financial barriers in pursuing their dreams at VinUni. These scholarships are generously funded by dedicated donors, with selection criteria determined by each scholarship fund.

- Dean Choi Grant by Soosan (10% of tuition)
  For students of the College of Business & Management (CBM) and the College of Engineering & Computer Science (CECS). This scholarship supports outstanding young talents, encouraging them to boldly pursue academic excellence and innovation.
- Future Leader (10% of tuition) – For students across all Colleges. This scholarship honors young individuals who aspire to become the next generation of leaders – those with vision, resilience, and a commitment to creating positive impact in their communities.

#### HOLISTIC ADMISSIONS CRITERIA – AACC

VinUniversity evaluates applicants holistically across four AACC criteria (A – Outstanding Ability: Exceptional academic aptitude; A – Aspiration: Strong passion and ambition; C – Creativity: Innovative and creative thinking; C – Commitment: Resilience and determination). The Admissions Committee does not rely solely on exam scores or GPA. Therefore, no matter your background or circumstances, if you demonstrate aspiration – intellect – resilience, you can conquer a VinUniversity’s scholarship.

#### OPPORTUNITY FOR “DUAL SCHOLARSHIPS”

In addition to Merit-based Scholarships, applicants may also receive other incentive or special scholarships, such as: Women-In-Tech Scholarship, Dean Choi Grant by Soosan colongmerate, Future Leader Scholarship, etc.

#### A LONG-TERM SUPPORT COMMITMENT

Scholarships are applied throughout the entire undergraduate program, provided students meet the minimum renewal requirements. Notably, during their studies, if students demonstrate exceptional academic or research performance or initiate impactful projects, they may be considered for additional progress of scholarships or achievement awards.

As long as you strive and deserve it, VinUniversity is willing to award you valuable scholarships!
