# Scholarships and Financial Support for Current Students

## I. Financial Assistance & Grant

### 1. Bursary

**Value**

5% to 65% of tuition

**Award Tenure**

Semester/Academic Year

**Purpose**

To provide additional financial support for students facing unexpected financial hardship- such as sudden loss of family income, medical emergencies, or other unforeseen circumstances – ensuring that their education at VinUni is not disrupted due to financial challenges.

**Eligibility**

- Be a current full-time VinUni student in good academic and disciplinary standing.
- Experience verified unexpected financial hardship (e.g., sudden loss of income, medical emergencies, or other unforeseen events).
- Demonstrate the financial impact on their ability to continue studying at VinUni.
- Submit a complete Financial Aid application with all required supporting documents.

**When to Apply?**

Applications are reviewed twice annually, in July and November.

**How to Apply?**

Detailed application guidelines and timelines are available [HERE].

**Note**

This bursary is supplementary support, intended to assist students *after enrollment*. It is independent from entry scholarships or initial financial aid packages granted at admission.

### 2. (ACCESS) Future Leader Grant

**Value**

10% tuition fee, equivalent to:

- Nursing program: VND 34,965,000/ Academic year
- Other UG program: VND 81,585,000/ Academic year

**Award Tenure**

Full duration (renewable yearly based on eligibility & performance)

**Purpose**

- Each year, outstanding students come close to achieving their dream of studying at VinUni, but the final 10%-20% of tuition remains an obstacle their families cannot overcome.
- The Future Leader Grant removes this last barrier, opening the door for deserving young people to study at VinUni, thrive, realize their potential, and become impactful future leaders.

**Eligibility**

Applicants must satisfy ALL conditions below to be considered:

- Have received a VinUni merit-based scholarship covering 80% or more of tuition.
- Demonstrate verified financial hardship, indicating inability to cover the remaining 10–20% of tuition.
- Have completed and submitted the Financial Aid Application with all required documents.

**When to Apply**

Applicants may apply for the Future Leader Grant after receiving their VinUni scholarship notification and before the official enrollment deadline of the academic year.

- Applications submitted after the enrollment deadline will only be considered in exceptional circumstances with valid justification.
- The Grant is processed on a rolling basis until available funding is fully allocated.

**How to Apply**

Applicants must complete the following steps:

- Submit a Financial Aid Application via the designated VinUni application portal.
- Provide required supporting documents per requested by the University for validation (Link for guidance).
- Undergo financial assessment and eligibility review conducted by the authorized University committee.
- Applicants will be notified of the final decision in writing once the review process is completed (normally within 15 working days from the date of submission).

**Required Documents**

Please refer to HERE.

**Selection Criteria**

- Verified financial hardship preventing payment of remaining 10–20% tuition.
- Strong motivation to enroll if supported.
- Priority given to cases with stronger leadership potential (higher scholarship) first and and then to cases with more significant hardship

*\* Academic readiness/AACC attributes is already established through the merit-based scholarship award and will not be reassessed.*

**Expected deliverables/ requirement to continue to receive the funding**

- Maintain main entry scholaship (min cGPA 2.5). No violations of academic integrity.
- Maintain Full-Time Enrollment Status (not applicable for leave of absence year).
- Uphold the spirit of the grant, by actively contribute to the VinUni community through participation in student activities, volunteer work, leadership roles, or community engagement.
- Write a letter of appreciation to the donor at the end of the year
- Attend an annual donor appreciation event

*\* The cGPA requirement for retaining the combined scholarship/grant package shall follow the same retention standards as the Merit-based Scholarship.*

**Grant Revocation & Refund**

Not applicable

### 3. (SHINE) Student Shine Grant

**Value

*National:***
Upto VND 5,000,000/ student/ event
Upto VND 15,000,000/ team/ event

***International:***
Upto VND20,000,000/ student/ event
Upto VND40,000,000/ team/ event

**Award Tenure**

One-off per approved competition/event

**Purpose**

To enable future leaders to shine by providing them with opportunities and support to showcase their talent and excellence at national and international competitions, conferences, and professional platforms.

**Eligibility**

Applicants must meet all of the following conditions:

- Be a current full-time VinUni student in good academic and disciplinary standing;
- Have been officially selected, invited, or qualified to participate in a national or international competition, conference, or equivalent showcase platform;
- Demonstrate clear preparation, commitment, and readiness to represent VinUni professionally;
- Provide documentation verifying participation or qualification.

**When to Apply**

- Apply once official confirmation of acceptance/participation is received;
- Submit at least 10 working days before the departure date;
- Late applications reviewed only in exceptional cases.

**How to Apply**

- Attach all required documents.
- Undergo review by the authorized committee.
- Receive written notification of approval once review is completed.
- The result will be available within 5–7 working days (from the date when all required documents are fully submitted).

**Required Documents**

- Official invitation/acceptance/qualification letter;
- Proposal outlining purpose, role in the event, and expected outcomes;
- Estimated budget (registration, travel, accommodation, materials);
- Any competition guidelines or event details.
- Statement of motivation & expected impact

**Selection Criteria**

- Legitimacy and prestige of the national/international event or platform;
- Student’s demonstrated readiness and potential to excel or contribute meaningfully:
- Potential positive impact on the student’s growth and development – aligned with EXCEL attributes;
- Professional representation of VinUni.

**Expected deliverables/ requirement to continue to receive the funding**

1. Provide Proof of Participation: Submit confirmation of attendance, participation certificate, or competition/conference completion proof.

2. Submit a Post-Event Reflection/Report: A brief report (1–2 pages) summarizing key learnings, outcomes, achievements, and how the experience contributed to personal or academic development.

3. Share Outputs/Deliverables (if applicable), i.e: presentation slides, posters, reserach abstracts, project outcomes, competition results.

4. Represent VinUni Professionally: Maintain appropriate behavior and uphold VinUni’s reputation throughout the event.

5. Acknowledge VinUni Support (as required): Include VinUni name/logo in presentations, posters, or profiles when appropriate and permitted by event guidelines.

6. Contribute to VinUni Outreach & Donor Engagement:

- Provide content (photos, short quotes, or a brief testimonial) that may be used for VinUni’s communication channels, upon request.
- Write a thank-you letter to the donor at the end of the year.
- Attend the annual donor appreciation event (if invited).

**Grant Revocation & Refund**

If the student does not participate in the approved activity/event for any reason other than documented exceptional circumstances, the full amount of the Shine Grant must be returned to the University.

### 9. Start-up Grant

**Value**

Up to 45 mil VND/project

**Award Tenure**

One – off per request

**Purpose**

The fund is established to provide essential financial resources that foster innovation activities among students, faculty, and researchers at VinUniversity (collectively referred to as Founders), and to support the realization of projects with the potential to create significant economic and social value.

**Eligibility**

- Startup teams must include at least two (02) members who are current undergraduate or graduate students, alumni, faculty, or researchers at VinUniversity, serving as Founders or Co-Founders.
- Each Founder may only apply for funding for one (01) project.
- If a Founder withdraws from the project midway, the project team must notify E-Lab via email at least two weeks in advance and submit a transition plan that designates a Co-Founder or another team member to take over the project leadership role. Failure to comply with this requirement may result in the termination of funding.

**When to apply?**

Rolling review until funds are fully allocated.

**How to apply?**

- Startups prepare a project proposal (including project description, activity plan, and financial plan). The financial plan must comply with the budget utilization principles.
- The Evaluation Committee reviews and selects qualified proposals.
- Funding decisions are announced (including the approved amount and the required project commitments).

**Required Documents**

- A project proposal (including project description, activity plan, and financial plan).
- The financial plan must adhere to the budget utilization principles.

**Selection Criteria**

- Practicality and value of the solution
- Clarity of the implementation plan
- MVP & PoC (Minimum Viable Product & Proof of Concept)
- Reasonable and transparent budgeting.

**Expected deliverables/ requirement to continue to receive the funding**

Not applicable, as this type of funding is granted only once per recipient.

**Grant Revocation & Refund**

- If a Founder withdraws from the project midway, the project team must notify E-Lab via email at least two weeks in advance and submit a transition plan that designates a Co-Founder or another team member to assume the project leadership role. Failure to comply with this requirement may result in the termination of funding.
- This grant is intended solely for purposes directly related to the startup project and will be disbursed based on the objectives and deliverables agreed upon between the Founder and the Investment Committee. Any use of funds that deviates from the committed objectives may result in the expenditure being disallowed and the grant being reclaimed.
- The funding may not be used for personal expenses such as project members’ salaries, housing rent, utilities, living costs, or any other personal expenditures not directly related to the project. E-Lab reserves the right to suspend or revoke funding if any violations are detected.

For more information about the Start-up Grant and the Entrepreneurship Lab, please kindly visit HERE.
