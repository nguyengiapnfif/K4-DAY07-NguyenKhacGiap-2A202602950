# Student Loans

## Student loans

VinUniversity offers a Student Loan Program (in collaboration with banks), providing additional financial support for students who need flexible payment options during their study period.

Key highlights of the program:

1. Loan amount up to 100% of tuition fees
2. Fixed preferential interest rate of only 8%/year for the first 4 years (For certain special cases, VinUni may provide additional interest support, allowing students to pay as low as 4%/year for up to 4 years, depending on the university’s evaluation criteria and available support budget)
3. Loan term up to 10 years
4. No collateral required
5. Simple procedures and fast approval process
6. Students can start repayment after graduation

This program serves as a practical financial solution that helps reduce the immediate financial burden on families while expanding access to education for talented students facing financial challenges.

For any inquiries or assistance with the financial support request process, students can contact the Financial Aid Office.
